Research Portfolio & Projects

Click on a topic panel below to expand and view ongoing projects, methodologies, and experimental configurations.

Stratified Flows

Investigation of gravity currents, particle-laden gravity currents, and turbulent entrainment processes. This research explores underlying geophysical phenomena like oceanic overflows, sub-surface turbidity currents, and powder avalanches.

Multiphase Flows

Focusing on particle-laden distributions, turbulent bubbly flow physics, gas-liquid phase boundaries, and hydrodynamic cavitation instabilities. This builds directly upon experimental frameworks established during my work on re-entrant flows at TU Delft.

Advanced Flow Diagnostics

Utilizing high-energy X-ray diagnostics, X-ray densitometry/tomography, and classical laser-based fluid flow tracking mechanics (such as Particle Image Velocimetry - PIV, PLIF, and Particle Tracking Velocimetry - PTV) to resolve dense or hidden fluid structures.
